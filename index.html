Privacy Policy

Apps by Sabin Khanal · Last updated: 9 October 2026

Expense TrackerEMI Calculator NotesBlock Drop Puzzle

Summary

These apps do not collect, share, or sell any personal data. They have no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and they request no device permissions. They do not connect to the internet.

Everything you enter stays only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently. I have no access to it.

Expense Tracker

  • Stores the expenses you enter (title, amount, category, date) on your device only.
  • Collects or transmits no data.

EMI Calculator

  • Remembers your last loan inputs on your device so you don't have to retype them.
  • Collects or transmits no data.

Notes

  • Stores your notes and their creation times on your device only.
  • Collects or transmits no data.

Block Drop Puzzle

  • Stores your best score on your device only.
  • Collects or transmits no data.

Children

The apps collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If an app ever starts collecting data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
